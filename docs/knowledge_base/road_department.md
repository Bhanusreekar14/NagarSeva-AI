# Roads & Infrastructure Department Services

## Scope of Work
The Roads Department handles:
- Potholes and asphalt surface damage
- Longitudinal, transverse, and alligator pavement cracks
- Damaged sidewalks, curbs, and bridge maintenance
- Road resurfacing and traffic barrier repairs

## Resolution Timelines
- **High Severity Potholes**: 24 to 48 hours
- **Medium Crack Repair**: 3 to 5 business days
- **Major Road Resurfacing**: 7 to 14 business days
