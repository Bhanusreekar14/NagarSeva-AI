# Sanitation & Waste Management Services

## Scope of Work
The Sanitation Department is responsible for:
- Garbage collection and waste bin clearance
- Illegal dumping removal (plastics, metals, cardboard, hazardous waste)
- Street sweeping and public bin maintenance
- Littering enforcement and recycling management

## Reporting Illegal Dumping
To report illegal dumping, upload a photo or submit a description through NagarSeva AI. The issue is assigned a High priority if plastic or non-biodegradable hazardous material is detected.
