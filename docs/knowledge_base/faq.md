# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I track my complaint?
A: Enter your unique tracking ID (e.g., NGS-2026-000001) in the NagarSeva AI portal to view live status updates.

Q: Which department handles streetlights?
A: The Electricity Department manages all public streetlights and power pole maintenance.

Q: How long does pothole repair take?
A: High-severity potholes are repaired within 24 to 48 hours of ticket confirmation.

Q: How do I report illegal dumping?
A: Submit a photo or text complaint via NagarSeva AI. The system routes it directly to the Sanitation Department.
