# Storm Water & Flood Response Services

## Scope of Work
The Storm Water Department handles:
- Severe street flooding and waterlogging
- Blocked storm drains and clogged catch basins
- Drain overflows during heavy rainfall

## Emergency Flood Support
Waterlogging issues are flagged as **Critical** priority in NagarSeva AI. Response teams are dispatched within 2 hours during rainy season alerts.
