# Municipal Complaint Process Guide

## How to Report an Issue
Citizens can report civic issues through NagarSeva AI using either image upload or text descriptions.
1. **Photo Upload**: Take a picture of the issue (e.g. pothole, uncollected garbage, waterlogging). The vision model automatically detects the damage and identifies the category.
2. **Text Description**: Describe the problem in simple words. The NLP engine classifies the issue and assigns priority.

## Complaint Tracking
Every submitted complaint receives a unique tracking ID in the format `NGS-YYYY-XXXXXX` (e.g., `NGS-2026-000001`).
Citizens can check complaint status anytime using the tracking ID.

## Complaint Lifecycle States
- **Pending**: Complaint received and routed to department.
- **Assigned**: Inspection team assigned.
- **In Progress**: Repair/cleanup work active on site.
- **Resolved**: Work verified and completed.
- **Closed**: Complaint ticket closed.
