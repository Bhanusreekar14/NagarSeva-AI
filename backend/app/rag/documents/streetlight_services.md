# Electricity & Streetlight Services

## Scope of Work
The Electricity Department manages:
- Non-functional or flickering streetlights
- Damaged electric light poles
- Exposed wiring and transformer hazards
- Power outages affecting public street lights

## Resolution Timelines
- Exposed Wiring Hazard: Emergency response within 4 hours
- Single Streetlight Repair: 24 to 48 hours
